## Accessibility observations

- **Mobility:** All stops reachable by wheelchair; no steps or curbs on route.
- **Vision:** One player with low vision used phone camera zoom to scan QR; worked well.
- **Reading:** Puzzle text at Stop 4 assumed grade-4 math; younger kids needed adult help.
- **Sensory:** No issues; audio cues (hoot sound) were subtle and not startling.

## Verdict

- [x] Pass with fixes (list below)
- [ ] Pass — ship it
- [ ] Fail — rework and retest

**Fixes required:**

1. **Stop 4 puzzle difficulty:** Digit-sum is too hard for the 6–10 age band. Consider splitting into two difficulty tiers (e.g., "count the numbers" for younger kids vs. "add the digits" for older).
2. **Stop 4 QR placement:** Sign was shaded by tree canopy. Raise to 5.5 ft or move 2 ft left for better sun exposure.
3. **Stop 2 hint wording:** "Only count the seats looking at the oak" was ambiguous — one team counted all benches in the park. Reword to "Count only the benches that face the oak tree."

## Additional notes

- The cozy/spooky toggle worked well; families stayed cozy, teens asked "what does spooky say?"
- Service worker cached correctly; one team lost signal mid-game and progress was preserved.
- The start poster QR (access code WARMUP) scanned instantly on all devices.
- Teams naturally helped each other at Stop 4 — consider whether that's OK for competitive play or if spacing should be wider.
