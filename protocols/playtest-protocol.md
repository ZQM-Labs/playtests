# Playtest Protocol (standard)

Run this for every edition before it ships, and for every pack change after it.

## Roles

- **Game Master (1):** runs the clock, observes, does NOT help. Holds the
  answer key.
- **Players (2–4 teams of 2–5):** ideally people who haven't seen the answers.
  Mix ages if the edition targets families.
- **Scribe (1, optional):** timing sheet + quotes. The Game Master can scribe
  for small tests.

## Run-of-show

1. **Brief (5 min):** read the 30-second briefing from the organizer toolkit.
   Start the clock.
2. **Play (30–60 min):** teams roam. The Game Master follows at a distance,
   notes where teams hesitate, and records every hint request. No helping —
   this is the test.
3. **Tally (10 min):** gather at the finale. Score, tiebreaker, group photo.
4. **Debrief (15 min):** ask each team: hardest stop? easiest? anything
   confusing? would you pay for this? Write down verbatim quotes.

## What to observe (the real test)

- **Scan failures:** any QR that took >2 seconds. Fix placement, not the QR.
- **Puzzle fairness:** a stop nobody solved without hint 3 has a bad puzzle —
  rewrite it.
- **Pacing:** any leg over 6 minutes walking feels dead; tighten the route.
- **GPS drift:** note where the 30 m check struggled (tree cover, buildings).
- **Energy:** where did teams laugh? Where did they check their watches? Double
  the first, cut the second.

## Pass criteria

- All 6 QRs scan in ≤2 seconds on the re-walk.
- Every stop solved by at least one team without the Ranger assist.
- Total runtime 30–60 minutes.
- At least one verbatim "that was fun" in the debrief.
